## Support & Troubleshooting

### Can't Login?
1. Double-check email address (case-sensitive)
2. Verify password (check for typos)
3. Ensure account was created by admin
4. Try clearing browser cache
5. Contact info@fm8.global

### Forgot Password?
1. Admin (info@fm8.global) can reset in User Management
2. Ask admin to generate new temporary password
3. Login with new password
4. Change to personal password immediately

### Can't See Predictions/Outcomes?
1. Ensure you're logged in (check top-right corner)
2. Your account must be "Approved User" or "Admin"
3. Contact info@fm8.global to verify your role
